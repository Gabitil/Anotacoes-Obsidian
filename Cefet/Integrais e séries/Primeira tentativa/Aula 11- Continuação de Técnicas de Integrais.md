$f(x) \times g(x)=\int f'(x)g(x)dx +\int f(x)g'(x)dx$

$\int f(x)g'(x)dx=f(x) \times g(x)-\int g(x)f'(x)dx$

$u=f(x)\implies du=f'(x)dx$
$v=g(x) \implies dv=g'(x)dx$
