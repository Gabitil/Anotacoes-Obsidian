### Exemplo:

Escreva o número $2,3272727\dots$ como uma fração

$2,3272727\dots=2,3+2,3272727\dots$
$\frac{23}{10}+0,027+0,00027+0,0000027+\dots$
$\frac{23}{10}+\frac{27}{10^3}+\frac{27}{10^5}+\frac{27}{10^7}+\dots$
$$
\frac{23}{10}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} {\frac{27}{10^3}\left( \frac{1}{10²} \right)^{n-1}}=\frac{23}{10}+\frac{\left( \frac{27}{10^3} \right)}{1-\frac{1}{10²}}
$$
$$
\frac{23}{10}+\left( \frac{\frac{27}{10^3}}{\frac{99}{10^2}} \right) = \frac{23}{10}+\frac{27}{10^3}\cdot \frac{10^2}{99}=\frac{23}{10}+\frac{27}{990}=\frac{2177+27}{990}=\frac{2304}{990}
$$
